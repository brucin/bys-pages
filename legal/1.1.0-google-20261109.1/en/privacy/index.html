1. Scope and controller

This policy covers the Google integration phase of Bailu Hub 1.1.0 and its existing free services on iPhone and iPad and the official website services described below. Our registered address is Room 504-15, No. 1388 Shuidian Road, Hongkou District, Shanghai 200434, China. Send privacy requests to contact@bailuhub.com. Our privacy team handles them under the oversight of the company principal.

2. Purposes, grounds and choices
Account authentication and your records support the service you request. Access controls, abuse prevention and necessary logs protect the service and meet applicable legal duties. Contractual necessity or legal obligation is used only for processing actually necessary for that purpose, not as a blanket basis for optional marketing or sharing sensitive content. Applicable grounds depend on the law of the relevant region.

Online AI sharing, optional app analytics and Apple online speech fallback have separate notices and choices. Separate or explicit consent is required for sensitive information such as health data where applicable. System permissions, sign-in, age declarations and reading this policy do not substitute for one another. Declining an optional feature leaves unrelated functions available; withholding necessary authentication or resource-request data affects the corresponding account or online service.

3. Accounts, sign-in and adult eligibility
Registration is available to people aged 18 or older in regions where we offer the service. When registering outside mainland China, declare your actual country or region, confirm that you are at least 18, and separately acknowledge that relevant data will be transferred to mainland China for processing. We record your declarations, the versions of the Privacy Policy and User Agreement you accept, and the time and source of confirmation to apply eligibility rules and keep a record of your choice. Store region, IP address and interface language cannot substitute for your declaration. Existing users receive a separate prompt when a new declaration is required.

You can sign in with Apple or email. We process internal account IDs, your verified Apple identifier, any name and email address you authorize Apple to share (including a relay address), email verification details, sessions and security records. Legacy password access may receive a phone number to locate an existing verified credential. Apple users may add a recovery email.

A linked identity returns you to your original account. With an unlinked identity, choose to verify and link your original account or explicitly create your first account. Linking requires verification of both identities and your confirmation. Matching email addresses do not merge accounts. After sign-out, reinstallation or a device change, verified server records establish account ownership. A locally remembered sign-in method is a hint. This phase preserves existing identity records and offers no new WeChat sign-in or linking. Google processing is described below. Full merging of existing accounts is not available.

Nicknames can sync. Body measurements and preset avatars remain on the device under your account. Information you choose to send in chat or feedback is processed for that feature.

Google sign-in, credentials and linking
Google sign-in is optional and, where activated, is offered to users outside mainland China, including Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan. Apple and email sign-in remain available; a sign-in method is not a paid entitlement. Google authorization starts only when you choose Google sign-in or use it to verify an account action.

Google sign-in uses the basic openid, email and profile scopes and may return a Google identifier, email address and basic profile. We do not request access to Gmail messages, Drive files, contacts or calendars. This implementation identifies accounts using the stable identifier verified by Google. It does not separately save the returned email, name or avatar as your Bailu Hub profile, and a Google email address does not automatically become a linked Bailu Hub email.

We process necessary Google identity proofs, account links and verification records for sign-in, explicit account linking and security. Identity identifiers are encrypted at rest and checked using digests. The original Google ID token is used for the current verification and safe recovery of the same request; it is not persisted as account profile data or written to business logs. The SDK may temporarily use the system Keychain during authorization. This app clears the SDK sign-in state when its callback finishes; this does not revoke your Google authorization.

We also retain a digest of the original credential to prevent duplicate execution of the same request, an encrypted journal of verified attempts, and linking, verification and session records. Google access and refresh tokens are not sent to our business backend. Necessary identity credentials are processed through the cloud and API transmission services described in sections 12 and 13. Google-derived identity data is not used as content for Baixiaosheng answers.

The Google Sign-In SDK may collect a user identifier and IP address for authorization records and fraud prevention; the IP address may also be used to estimate approximate location. Google processes its service data globally under its privacy policy. Bailu Hub currently provides its business-data service in mainland China. Choosing Google sign-in connects to Google and transfers necessary identity credentials to Bailu Hub; it does not mean all processing takes place in Shanghai.

Unlinking also requires recent authentication and verification of original-account ownership. Linking or unlinking updates our account-security state. You can manage links or request account deletion in Bailu Hub account security. At least one usable sign-in method must remain before unlinking. Unlinking in Bailu Hub, signing out, deleting a Bailu Hub account and removing a third-party connection in your Google Account are different actions. This implementation does not automatically revoke Google-side authorization. You can manage that connection separately in your Google Account; removing it does not automatically delete Bailu Hub training or chat data. Existing retention and deletion rules continue to apply, without a new fixed waiting period or an unverified promise about when every supplier copy is erased.

Google sign-in data in this implementation is used for authentication, explicit linking and security verification, not for sending training or chat content to Google, personalized advertising or marketing. Data handling for other Bailu Hub features remains governed by their corresponding sections and your choices.

Google explains its processing at https://policies.google.com/privacy . Manage Google-side connections at https://myaccount.google.com/connections .

4. Guests and device verification
Guests may preview the first movement and restricted models without signing in. Resource requests still involve limited authorization, network and necessary security records, and local model caching. Guest preview does not activate the camera, microphone or formal training, create anonymous cloud training history, or automatically merge preview data into account history. Guest local data may not survive clearing storage or changing devices.

Where a guest text AI experience is available, requesting it after the feature notice initiates Apple App Attest/DeviceCheck verification of app authenticity and trial usage. Processing includes an installation-ID digest, App Attest key ID, public key, attestation receipt, one-time challenges, signature counters, trial status and timestamps. Raw DeviceCheck tokens are used for communication with Apple, not as business-ledger fields; device private keys are not uploaded. IP addresses may support risk checks. Advertising IDs, contacts and camera images are not used to identify guests.

Device verification does not identify a unique natural person or provide AI-sharing consent. Failed verification or unavailable allowance can prevent a new AI request, but should not block ordinary preview.

A completed guest answer has a 24-hour retrieval window. Proof challenges last at most 120 seconds and no longer than the original permit. These are retrieval or verification periods, not proof that question ledgers, device attestations, anti-abuse markers or Apple-held data are erased at expiry, nor that the experience allowance has reset.

5. Practice, chat, plans and feedback
We process selected courses and movements, practice times and completion, plans and changes, and the sensations, questions, chat, suggestions and attachments you submit to save, synchronize and show your records. Drafts, caches and pending records may be local to an account; sending or synchronizing transfers them to our servers. Authorized support personnel have access only as needed for the function.

Descriptions of bodily sensations can contain sensitive health information. Provide only what is needed for the practice or question, not other people’s medical records, identity information or unrelated sensitive details. Feedback images and diagnostic attachments require submission through the relevant flow; camera permission or a report feature does not automatically upload all screens or recordings.

Personal records let you view your training and feedback. The 28-day window governs when new training feedback may be added. It runs for 28 × 24 hours from the original training start time; no new related feedback can be added at or after expiry, and viewing old records does not restart it. It is not a deletion deadline for training history, chats or backups, and expiry does not establish erasure. The separate My Plan feature and initiation of AI structured plans are not available in this release. Existing plans, drafts and history retain their original ownership and data handling.

6. Camera and on-device processing
Human-presence and pose detection use Apple Vision on the device. Gesture controls use the bundled MediaPipe hand model locally. Person association distinguishes the current operator rather than verifying identity. This recognition flow does not upload raw camera frames or skeletons to our servers or Google’s cloud. Practice-completion facts may still synchronize as service records.

Relevant functions request iOS camera permission, which can be withdrawn in Settings. Without it, visual recognition is unavailable; supported touch controls remain an alternative. Images you choose to send are separate submissions, so local recognition does not mean that attachments can never be uploaded.

7. Speech input and prepared audio
Mandarin, Cantonese and English speech input needs microphone and speech-recognition permissions and prefers available on-device recognition. Apple online speech fallback is off by default; audio may be sent to Apple only when you separately allow it and the system supports it. You can instead use available local recognition, touch or text. Apple’s system notice and privacy information govern its online processing; it is not represented as confined to Shanghai or mainland China.

Training-command audio is processed in memory without creating a recording file in that flow. Chat speech becomes text, and ending push-to-talk may send it; sent text is handled as chat data. Withdrawing permission stops subsequent input but does not erase text already sent.

Prepared training prompts use company-written scripts and preset voices through the official Alibaba Cloud Model Studio API, not user recordings or voice cloning. Generated AI audio is bundled with the app or downloaded and cached through controlled OSS/CDN resources. Playback does not send your recordings or chat to the production service or synthesize audio again on every play. This production setup is separate from Apple online speech recognition that a user separately permits; the production location does not establish where user speech is processed.

Before using online AI questions, you will see a separate sharing notice. Guest questions do not access member training history. The provider describes the route as processing in mainland China. The shared route uses server-side safety and output filters. External CIP inspection is disabled on this route.

We record the authorization version, request identifier, time and status to verify your choice, prevent replay and retrieve results. Withdrawing in AI consent management stops new calls. A request already in progress may finish. You can separately request deletion of history. This agreement gives us no general license to publish your content or use it for model training. The model service processes requests, results and necessary metadata. Contact the privacy email to request deletion. We will coordinate with the relevant provider and explain the outcome.

Our cloud account lists North China 2 (Beijing) as the billing region for pay-as-you-go qwen-plus inference. The current Alibaba Cloud Model Studio service agreement names 通义云启（杭州）信息技术有限公司 as the provider. Under that agreement the provider processes business data on instructions and for necessary content safety checks. Training requires authorization. Retention is limited to the necessary period, and use of backups pending deletion is restricted. The processing region for a particular request depends on its service route.

9. Suggestion replies and notifications
With system notification permission, signing in or restoring a session can register an Apple APNs token with us along with an installation digest, app ID, environment, language, account/session/device binding, binding version and timestamps. Registration supports correct binding and delivery; it does not mean that a notification has been sent or give AI or analytics permission.

The this phase’s remote notification scope is an official reply to your own suggestion. Apple receives the device token and a minimal payload: a generic message, opaque suggestion and event IDs, fixed type/route, validation version and issue/expiry times. It excludes suggestion or reply text, bodily sensations, email, passwords and private-resource links. Opening details requires checking the current account and ownership.

Declining or disabling notifications does not block practice. Sign-out, account changes or permission withdrawal initiate unbinding; network failures may require retries, and server receipts determine status. Local reminders are distinct from remote delivery. Notifications can be delayed or fail and are not an emergency channel.

Authenticated events can be linked to your account. These fields exclude chat text, raw audio and video, skeletal data, email addresses and phone numbers. Withdrawal stops collection, clears the local sending queue and sends a withdrawal request to the server. Necessary training records are kept separately.

Our mainland website homepage automatically loads Baidu Tongji. The international homepage automatically loads Google Analytics 4. Neither homepage offers an on-site analytics withdrawal control. Providers may process cookies, IP addresses, visits and interactions, device details and region. You can manage cookies in your browser and contact us about historical data. Baidu Tongji is provided by 北京百度网讯科技有限公司, reachable at ext_tongji_reply@baidu.com. Its policy describes storage in China and legally governed cross-border cases.

Our GA4 property retains events for 2 months and user data for 14 months, resetting the user-data period on new activity. Standard aggregated reports and backups are handled separately. Google signals and user-provided data collection are disabled. Regional controls permit granular location and device collection and advertising personalization in all listed regions. No Google Ads account is linked. Account sharing is enabled for modeling and business insights using aggregated and de-identified measurement data, technical support and business recommendations. Sharing for Google products and services is off.

14. Retention and deletion
We retain account details and your training, plan and chat history while providing your personal history service. Section 15 describes how we handle a valid deletion request. Records may remain on our servers after you sign out or uninstall the app.

Our retention targets vary by category. We aim to clear suggestion text, official replies and related support emails 90 days after case closure. If a case reopens, the period starts again when it closes. For screenshots the limit is the earlier of 90 days after submission or 30 days after resolution or closure. Diagnostic packages have a 90-day target from case creation. For optional app analytics, the targets are 30 days from receipt for raw events and batches and 90 days for consent commands. Current consent status and personal training history are retained separately.

Provider copies, mail archives, security logs and backups are handled according to their purposes, recovery needs, contracts and applicable law. Where retention is necessary, we restrict use and explain the basis and progress to the requester. Recorded deletion instructions must be reapplied to the relevant account when a backup is restored. Contact the privacy email to ask about progress and the basis for any continued retention.

Support staff review cases due for cleanup each week. Operations staff handle the business records, and email administrators handle the corresponding mail copies. They record the outcome. Operations staff also review overdue and failed cleanup of screenshots, diagnostics and optional analytics each week and follow up on outstanding items.

Our privacy team responds within 15 working days after identity verification. For complex requests, we explain the reason, progress and outstanding steps. Applicable statutory requirements take precedence. You may complain to the competent regulator or seek a remedy in a court with jurisdiction.

Staff coordinate deletion within the verified scope of your account. They mark each step complete after receiving a verifiable record of its outcome and follow up on failed steps. Access to temporarily retained copies is restricted and reviewed. Deletion records are kept separately from backups. Before restoring access after a backup recovery, staff reapply and check the recorded deletions.
