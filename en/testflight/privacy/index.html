1. Scope and operator

This policy applies only to invited internal testing of the Bailu Practice (白研社) iOS app through Apple TestFlight. This round is limited to the designated account holder personally, with no external invitations or public app distribution. It does not replace the applicable website, waitlist or future public-app policies. Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English interfaces do not establish regional availability.

The operator is Shanghai Bailuxing Data Technology Co., Ltd. (上海白鹿行数据科技有限公司), Room 504-15, No. 1388 Shuidian Road, Hongkou District, Shanghai, China, postal code 200434. The company privacy contact receives requests under the company lead at contact@bailuhub.com.

Formal practice and online AI in this round are limited to the invited tester aged 18 or older using the service in mainland China, excluding Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan. Ordinary first-lesson previews do not enable formal practice or online AI.

2. Accounts, devices and available entry points

This round uses an authorized existing username-and-password account. We process account identifiers, authentication results, sessions, device or installation identifiers and necessary security records to authenticate access, prevent replay and retrieve your own history. Do not include passwords, verification codes or private keys in feedback or email.

New Sign in with Apple, guest AI, remote push and complete self-service account deletion are not activated features of this round. New WeChat login is disabled. Any later activation requires an updated explanation and applicable choices; this policy does not activate those services. Existing identity links or code do not establish a new authorization request to a provider.

Guests may only browse first-lesson movements and restricted models, without camera, microphone or formal training records. Browsing produces necessary resource-authorization, network and security records and may cache models locally. Existing history, drafts and device keys retain their original account ownership; matching email addresses do not cause automatic merging. Nicknames synchronize to the server; current body measurements and preset avatars remain locally account-scoped unless you separately submit them in a message.

Reply-push delivery being unavailable does not mean notification registration has not occurred. While you are signed in and system notification permission is allowed, including after restoring sign-in or rechecking existing permission, the app may request an APNs device token from Apple and attempt to submit the token, installation identifier, app identifier, push environment and interface language to our server. When accepted, the server links this information to the current account, session and device and retains a digest of the installation identifier, the token and its digest, binding status and version, and necessary timestamps for correct binding, subsequent notification delivery and unbinding. Permission or successful registration does not mean a notification has been sent; this does not enable reply delivery in this round. Declining notifications does not affect practice. When permission is disabled, you sign out or you switch accounts, the app requests binding revocation through the corresponding flow. Network failures may require retries; actual handling is determined by the server receipt.

3. Practice, plans, conversations and feedback

To save, synchronize and display your records, we process selected lessons and movements, practice timing and completion, plans and changes, physical feelings, questions, conversations, suggestions and attachments you choose to submit. Unsent drafts, caches and pending synchronization records may remain on the device; submitted or synchronized content reaches our server.

Physical feelings may include sensitive health information. Supply only what this test needs and avoid other people’s information, medical records or identity documents. Sensitive processing and online AI sharing require the specific notice and separate choices at the relevant entry point; this policy does not replace required separate consent. Declining optional sharing should not affect unrelated basic functions.

4. Camera and on-device recognition

Formal practice uses on-device Apple Vision for person presence and pose, and a bundled MediaPipe hand model for fist-and-palm and air controls. Operator association distinguishes the controller without identifying a real person. This recognition flow does not upload camera frames or skeletons to our servers or Google Cloud.

iOS requests camera permission for the relevant feature; you may withdraw it in system settings. The corresponding visual recognition then becomes unavailable. Business facts such as practice completion may still synchronize. Screenshots or attachments you actively submit are separate from camera recognition.

5. Voice input and synthesized prompts

Mandarin, Cantonese and English voice input require microphone and speech-recognition permissions, preferring supported on-device recognition. Apple online fallback is off by default. Audio may reach Apple only after your separate allowance and where supported. You may decline fallback and use available local recognition, touch or text. Apple’s system notice and privacy information apply to its online service.

Raw training-command recognition audio is handled in memory without creating recording files in that path. Conversation speech becomes text; releasing push-to-talk can trigger sending. Sent text follows the conversation-data rules. Withdrawing permission stops corresponding new input but does not automatically erase sent text.

Fixed teaching prompts are AI-generated audio produced from company-authored scripts through Alibaba Cloud Model Studio production APIs using preset voices, not tester recordings or voice cloning. They are bundled or downloaded through controlled asset delivery and cached. Playback does not send your recordings or conversations to the voice-production service or synthesize each prompt again.

6. Baixiaosheng and online AI

Before using available account AI, review and separately choose AI data sharing. Depending on the entry point, our server sends your text or transcription, necessary conversation and practice context, feelings or goals, course knowledge and system instructions to answer or suggest practice guidance. AI consent is separate from sign-in, camera, analytics and notification choices.

The verified production AI route uses the Tongyi family through Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, received by Tongyi Yunqi (Hangzhou) Information Technology Co., Ltd., with mainland-China Beijing access. The actual model and scope appear in the pre-send disclosure. The provider retains requests and results for necessary purposes and applicable law without a single public fixed period; this is not zero retention. Provider changes require corresponding notice and choices. Guest AI is unavailable in this round.

Withdraw AI sharing in the app to stop new calls. Already authorized requests may complete; saved history is not automatically erased. Free accounts receive up to three successfully answered independent questions per Shanghai calendar day; one multi-question message may use multiple units. AI can be wrong and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

7. Optional app analytics

Our optional product analytics are off by default. Separate permission enables allowed events to improve reliability and practice flows. Events contain limited types, numbers, versions and random event or session identifiers, not conversation text, raw audiovisual data, skeletons, email or phone numbers. Authentication can link them to an account, so they are pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous.

The account-scoped local queue is capped at 200 events, 512 KiB and seven days. Withdrawal stops new collection and clears the pending local queue; pending server action is displayed until confirmed. Clearing optional events does not remove necessary practice facts. This choice does not control Apple TestFlight collection described next.

8. Apple TestFlight

Apple TestFlight automatically collects beta crash and usage information and shares it with us. Email invitations may associate your name and email; TestFlight offers no separate opt-out. Submitted feedback and screenshots reach Apple and us. We use this information to improve the app and address test issues, without sharing it with third parties. Apple’s processing and rights channels are explained at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/. You may stop testing in TestFlight; stopping does not erase previously collected data.

9. Suggestions, support and external pages

When you submit suggestions, bug reports or email, we process account associations, contact addresses, text, screenshots and diagnostic attachments to investigate, reply and track resolution. Review submissions to exclude passwords, credentials and irrelevant third-party information. Necessary providers support email delivery; the contact channel uses Tencent business email.

App and website analytics have separate choices; joining a website waitlist does not create an app account. Follow the relevant notices when leaving these internal-test legal pages for websites, waitlists or other sites. Accepting this policy does not authorize website Baidu Tongji, Google Analytics 4 or waitlist email. These test legal pages do not load optional analytics scripts.

10. Providers, storage and safeguards

Account and business requests use api.bailuhub.cn, with controlled assets at static.bailuhub.cn. Current backend and object storage use Alibaba Cloud services in mainland China. Authorization, private storage and expiring access URLs protect resources; a CDN domain does not make private files public. Planned AWS or regional architecture does not represent an active migration in this round.

Alibaba Cloud supplies current compute, database and asset/CDN infrastructure. Model Studio receives separately authorized AI requests and supplies the APIs used to produce fixed prompts. Apple supplies TestFlight and system services, and receives relevant audio if you separately allow online recognition. MediaPipe and Filament run locally; vendor names do not imply cloud upload. New WeChat authorization is disabled. We do not sell tester personal information or use it for advertising tracking.

We use transport protection, access controls, account-ownership checks and local file protection to limit staff and provider access to necessary purposes. No system guarantees absolute security; we will respond to information-security incidents and give legally required notices. New overseas recipients or processing arrangements require applicable notice and procedures before activation. Apple’s own international processing follows its service information; this policy does not state that all Apple data stays in mainland China.

11. Retention and deletion

Account details, practice, plans, conversations and suggestions are retained as needed for your history, synchronization and issue handling. Signing out, disabling AI or uninstalling does not erase them. There is no uniform day-based automatic expiry for all business data. When the purpose ends or a valid deletion request arrives, we act under applicable law and explain necessary retained categories, reasons, restrictions and progress to the requester.

Cleanup rules are 30 days for raw optional analytics events and batches, 90 days for analytics-consent commands, 90 days after upload for bug-report screenshots shortened to no later than 30 days after resolution or closure, and 90 days after ticket creation for diagnostic packages. Current model-access audit retention is 90 days. These are category-specific rules, not a universal period for records or backups or proof that every copy has been deleted. Failures and backlogs require further handling.

Local conversations and drafts are account-scoped; downloadable model/audio caches are distinct from personal business records. Clearing caches does not delete an account. Necessary legal receipts, security logs and backups are managed separately by purpose and applicable law. Immediate physical backup erasure is not promised; after a valid deletion request, further use for the original purpose is restricted and copies are disposed of when safely possible.

We retain received TestFlight issues and feedback only while needed for issue resolution, verification and necessary rights protection. No uniform automatic day-based period has been set. You may request deletion after leaving testing; Apple-held TestFlight data follows Apple’s rules and rights channels.

12. Your choices and rights

Use available app settings or contact contact@bailuhub.com to request access, copies, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal or account handling. Identity checks are proportionate to the request; never email passwords, full verification codes or private keys. We reply within 15 working days after identity verification, explaining reasons, progress and applicable timing for complex cases. This does not promise deletion of every copy within 15 working days.

Complete in-app self-service deletion for existing accounts is not available in this round. You may still request deletion or cessation through the contact email; testing does not remove these rights. Acceptance, login deactivation, sign-out or local erasure are not proof of complete deletion. We explain actual progress and necessary retention. This testing arrangement does not establish compliance of the future public app’s in-app deletion flow.

Control camera, microphone and system notification permissions in iOS Settings, and manage AI and our optional analytics separately. Withdrawal affects the relevant processing without retracting delivered feedback or unrelated permissions. Your right to complain to competent authorities or seek judicial remedies remains.

13. Content traceability and minors

Some pages use hexagram watermarks and necessary access audits to investigate abnormal extraction or redistribution. Watermarks do not directly display names, phone numbers or email, but server mappings may link them to accounts; they are not fully anonymous. Country/region preferences select layouts rather than proving nationality, residence or current service location. An older account’s CN default proves neither adulthood nor consent.

System recording or mirroring status may strengthen the watermark without reading recording files or guaranteeing screenshot/recording prevention. Trace identifiers are investigative leads requiring account-security and actual-record checks.

No tester under 18 is invited in this round; parental consent does not replace the age condition. Contact us if a minor uses an account or their information is collected in error, to request review, correction and appropriate action. Eligibility limits do not remove information rights or authorize arbitrary erasure of existing records.

After approval and actual publication, the page states its version and effective time. Expanded testing, new data uses or recipients, additional regions and public release require corresponding updates and applicable confirmation. Reading a draft or joining this round is not blanket consent to future changes or unreleased features.
