1. Operator and testing scope

These terms govern the single-tester invited internal TestFlight test of Bailu Practice iOS. The operator, address and contact details are in the matching Privacy Policy. This round is limited to the designated account holder aged 18 or older using the service in mainland China, excluding Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, without external invitations or public release. Three interface languages do not expand regional availability.

2. Functions in this round

This round tests first-lesson 3D browsing, practice after existing-account login with touch, voice and basic gestures, personal history, reflections, simple plans and available Baixiaosheng account AI. Devices, permissions, network and service conditions may affect use; testing does not guarantee defect-free operation or a fixed release date.

Payments, membership sales, offline courses, reservations/attendance, new extra practice, Bai meditation and new WeChat login are excluded from the first release. New Apple login, guest AI, remote push and complete self-service deletion are not represented as active in this round. Future paid or new functions need separate explanation; these terms do not authorize charges or activation.

3. Accounts and your records

Use only an authorized existing test account and protect its credentials. Do not impersonate others or access their records. Account details, device keys, drafts and history retain actual ownership and are not arbitrarily cleared for domain or version changes. Existing-account sign-in does not fabricate earlier legal consent or age declarations.

Guest browsing creates no formal practice record. Login is not AI, analytics or permission consent. Recovery, linking and data requests may require reasonable verification. Report test issues through feedback rather than treating data deletion as a default repair.

4. Practice safety

Demonstrations, practice prompts and AI replies support general learning, not medical diagnosis, treatment or rehabilitation prescriptions, and do not guarantee outcomes. Arrange a safe space and suitable intensity and rest. Stop if pain, dizziness or other discomfort occurs and seek appropriate professional help. Recognition, counting and speech can be wrong; do not force additional exercise because of them. This does not waive the operator’s legal duties.

5. Permissions and AI allowance

Camera, microphone, Apple online recognition, AI sharing and our optional analytics have separate choices. Refusal affects the relevant capability without waiving unrelated services or information rights. TestFlight collection follows the Privacy Policy and is not disabled by our analytics setting.

Free accounts have up to three successfully answered independent AI questions per Shanghai calendar day. A multi-question message may consume multiple units. Failure, retry and idempotency follow actual accounting; unlimited or guaranteed availability is not promised. Verify AI results yourself; they are not medical advice.

6. Content and intellectual property

You retain rights you lawfully hold in your content. We process it only as needed for requested storage, synchronization, display, support and separately authorized AI, without a blanket licence to publish, sell or repurpose it.

Lessons, text, software, brands, models and audio are protected by applicable rights. Fixed prompts are AI audio produced from company scripts through Alibaba Cloud Model Studio production APIs, not a live coach or platform endorsement. No provider model, preset-voice or trademark rights are transferred. Do not clone voices, resell synthesis services or bulk-extract, redistribute or sell protected content without authorization. Lawful uses remain protected.

7. Fair use and traceability

Do not attack systems, bypass asset authorization, steal credentials, collect others’ data or submit unlawful or infringing content. Ordinary personal practice, mirroring and necessary bug-report screenshots are not categorically prohibited. Do not remove trace identifiers to conceal unauthorized distribution of protected lessons, explanations or models.

Watermarks and audits are investigative leads, not automatic proof of infringement or grounds for punishment alone. Necessary restrictions on abnormal access will consider impact and provide appropriate explanation and appeal.

8. Third parties and test changes

Apple’s rules govern TestFlight installation, feedback and platform services; our processing is described in the matching Privacy Policy. Provider incidents or test maintenance may affect service. We will reasonably explain important changes and their effects on records. Source code, illustrations, plans and waitlist registration do not establish that a function is available.

9. Leaving testing and data requests

You may stop testing in TestFlight or withdraw relevant optional app permissions. Stopping, signing out, uninstalling, unlinking and deleting an account have different effects and do not erase all cloud or backup information. While complete self-service deletion is unavailable in this round, send access, correction or deletion requests to contact@bailuhub.com and receive an explanation of actual progress.

Ending testing does not remove accrued information rights, valid requests or legal obligations. Testing does not exclude liabilities that law makes non-excludable. Test material will not be published as user content without authorization.

Mainland Chinese law applies. You may contact support, complain to competent authorities or seek relief in a court with lawful jurisdiction; no mandatory arbitration is imposed.

The approved, actually published terms state their effective time. Expanded testing, regions, new uses and public release require updates and legally required confirmation. Continuing to browse a draft is not acceptance of future revisions.
