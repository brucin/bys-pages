Website campaign counts

2026-11-01 · Bailu Hub

We use separate campaign counts to understand which registered links bring readers to this website, which public pages they view, and how many waitlist requests complete email confirmation.

Visit counters store server time, a registered campaign ID, a page category and the owned website region. They do not store email addresses, confirmation tokens, full URLs, search terms, full referrers, device fingerprints or individual browsing histories.

A campaign source is held in the current browser tab for up to 30 minutes. Visits across devices or domains are not joined into one person. The footer control stops these additional campaign counters; source labels may still accompany an internal link for a waitlist request you choose to submit.

Visit counts are stored on our existing Alibaba Cloud server in mainland China for up to 30 days. Waitlist requests and confirmations retain their separate notice and retention rules. Aggregate reports are private to the operator and do not expose individual email records.

Entry visits are client-reported counts within a per-tab 30-minute window, not unique people. Network loss, browser restrictions, opt-out and non-human traffic affect coverage. Link labels do not authenticate the platform that displayed them.

Use the page footer to turn these campaign counts off or on. Existing homepage Google/Baidu analytics remain separate. Confirmation, withdrawal and legal pages do not load this counter script.
