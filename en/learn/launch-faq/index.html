Bailu Hub launch questions

The first version of Bailu Hub is planned as a free release on November 13, 2026, subject to review and regional availability. Download links will be added only after the release is confirmed.

Can I download it now?

Public downloads are not yet available. The planned date is not a statement that review has passed, or a promise of availability in every country and region.

What does a free first release mean?

The initial release is planned as a free version with free basic features. Courses, allowances and training access follow the released app. Any future optional monthly plans will be explained separately; joining the website list does not start a paid subscription.

Where will it be available?

The iPhone version will be distributed through the App Store in the countries and regions actually enabled for release. The website will provide the verified link. An Android release on the same date is not promised; the WeChat Mini Program entry is provided only on the Simplified Chinese site.

Is email signup an App Store pre-order?

No. Submit your email and confirm it through the message we send to request launch updates. This does not place an App Store pre-order, install the app automatically or grant access to training.

What if the confirmation email does not arrive?

Check your spam folder and the address you entered. If it is still missing, contact contact@bailuhub.com. Do not send passwords or verification codes by email.

Where can I follow updates?

Use the official website and release announcements as the reference. The official YouTube, Instagram and X accounts linked below also introduce the first lesson and the app flow.
